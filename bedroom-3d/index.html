Controls

  • Drag bed: move. Shift+drag bed: rotate.
  • Drag empty space: orbit. Right-drag: pan. Scroll: zoom.
  • ←↑→↓ nudge 1″ (Shift = 6″)
  • Q/E rotate 5°, R rotate 90°
  • Clearance is how far the bed can slide straight toward that side before it touches a wall.
  • Dashed arc: the entry door's swing.
