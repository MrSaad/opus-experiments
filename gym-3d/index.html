Gym walkthrough

A 1:1 model of the building gym. The long wall facing the treadmills is a mirror. Press Enter VR at the bottom on a Quest to walk around in it.

VR

  • Left stick: walk
  • Left stick click: sprint
  • Right stick: snap turn
  • Right stick click: smooth turn
  • A / X: jump
  • B / Y: respawn

Desktop

  • Mouse: look
  • WASD: walk
  • Shift: run
  • Space: jump
  • R: respawn
  • Touch: left side moves, right side looks
Esc frees the mouse · R respawns
